The Journey So Far

I didn't start as an endurance runner or adventurer. It started with a decision to step out of my comfort zone and see how far my legs could take me. Over the years, that curiosity transformed into marathons, multi-day cycling trips, and a constant drive to motivate others to seek their limits.
