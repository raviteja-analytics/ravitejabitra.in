Hi, I'm Ravi
Teja Bitra.

By day, I'm an IT professional. Outside of work, I'm someone who is obsessed with pushing personal limits — one discipline at a time.

I've been lifting weights for 4 years, picked up running just 4 months ago and already hooked, play Badminton whenever life allows, and hold my own in the pool — swimming is one of those things I genuinely love. The goal on the horizon? A full Triathlon. And after that, an Ultra Marathon, Hyrox, and somewhere in between — a serious Trekking expedition. I want to test what this body is really capable of.

On the travel front, I've explored a lot of South India and am slowly working through the North. Haven't stepped outside the country yet — but that chapter is coming, and I intend to make it worth the wait.

This space is my honest, ongoing journal — no filters, no flexing. Just real progress, real places, and real talk.
